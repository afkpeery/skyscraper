# 200Block — Skyscraper Edition

A retro-futuristic, rain-soaked night-city version of the 200Block site. Every section is a floor of one skyscraper, and the camera looks up at it from street level at roughly 45°.

- **Start:** zoomed out at street level. A bat-signal style searchlight projects a still of *The Last Collection* onto the clouds (click it to watch).
- **Navigation:** nav links, buttons, arrow keys and PageUp/PageDown send the elevator camera up or down to each floor. The wheel or a touch swipe climbs the tower.
- **Bricks:** every film thumbnail is spray-painted onto a brick. On hover the brick slides out like a Jenga block and the thumbnail plays as a GIF-style flipbook.
- **Easter egg:** a kid with a camcorder peeks around a corner on every floor.

Open `index.html` locally, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root).
The contact form opens the visitor's email app. Set your inbox with `data-to="you@example.com"` on `<form id="contactForm">`, or swap in Formspree or Netlify Forms.
