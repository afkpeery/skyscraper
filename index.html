Time Management

by Brandon Hawkins

“Believe what you see, never what they say.”

In development · Release date TBA

In a small town, everyone knows the story of Duncan Lane: the “boop” kid, the first minor ever wiped by the CIA, the cautionary tale of knowing too much too early. Three friends don’t buy it.

Hollywood is sure it’s all connected. Penny can’t shake the feeling she’s lived this before. Todd just wants everyone to stay out of trouble, even as his curiosity pulls him in. Their search leads to the TMA, an organization built on one family’s time technology and ruled by the unreadable Lord Zabius, and to the CIA men whose job is to make inconvenient truths disappear.

With a bureaucrat guarding the status quo and an agent torn between his orders and his code, the friends discover that the only thing worse than being wrong about what you saw is being right.
